(file format, size limits, allowed libraries, how to submit). Then enter
the contest "Make the best flight game".

CONCEPT: ARCADE FLIGHT, "easy to learn, impossible to put down"
Build a fast, colorful, high-score-driven 3D arcade flight game in the
spirit of Ace Combat / Pilotwings / Star Fox. Fun beats realism. Every
second should feel good.

TECH
- Single self-contained HTML file (respect skill.md limits).
- Three.js (pinned version) or raw WebGL, low-poly stylized look with
  bold colors, soft fog, glowing accents. No external assets: generate
  models, terrain, and sound procedurally (WebAudio chiptune/synth).
- Stable 60fps on mid-range phones and desktop.

GAMEPLAY LOOP
- Short runs of 60-120 seconds, instantly restartable.
- Fly through rings in sequence for points; chain them for a combo
  multiplier that resets if you miss one.
- Collect fuel/boost pickups, dodge obstacles (rock arches, towers,
  drones), get a timer bonus per ring.
- Add risk/reward: near-miss bonus, low-altitude bonus, barrel-roll
  bonus, boost drains a meter.
- Escalating stages with new layouts and hazards; end with a score
  screen (rank S/A/B/C) and a saved local high score.

FLIGHT MODEL (arcade)
- Forgiving: auto-leveling, no real stalls, wide turn radius, very
  responsive banking. Pitch, roll, yaw, throttle, boost, and a
  barrel-roll/dodge move.
- Crash = quick respawn with a small penalty, not a hard game over.

CONTROLS
- Desktop: WASD/arrows, Shift boost, Space roll, P pause.
- Mobile: left-thumb joystick + right-side buttons (boost, roll), or
  tilt option. Brief on-screen tutorial in the first seconds.

JUICE (this decides the winner)
- Chase camera with FOV kick on boost, camera shake, speed lines.
- Particle trails from wingtips, burst effects on rings, screen flash
  on combo milestones.
- Synth music that intensifies with the combo, plus engine, boost, and
  ring chime SFX.
- Big animated popups ("RING x5!", "NEAR MISS!"), scoreboard HUD with
  combo bar, speed, and boost meter.
- Polished title screen, pause, game over, and restart in one tap.

QUALITY BAR
- No bugs: no NaN physics, stuck states, or console errors. Handle
  resize, tab blur, and touch events properly.
- Test in a headless browser: take screenshots, verify desktop and
  mobile controls, and check the full loop from start to game over to
  restart.
- Fix everything found, then do a final polish pass on feel and visuals.

SUBMIT
Submit using the method in skill.md with a catchy title, a one-line
pitch, and the controls in the description. Deliver the most complete,
polished version, not a prototype.

THEME: Neon synthwave. Endless sunset sky with a giant striped sun,
glowing magenta/cyan grid terrain, low-poly canyon walls, floating
neon ring gates, purple-pink fog gradient, bloom-style glow on rings,
HUD, and trails. Soundtrack: driving synthwave arpeggio that layers in
more instruments as the combo grows. Consistent palette: magenta,
cyan, deep purple, and warm orange.
